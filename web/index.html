06 How to read it

Radar measures how strongly the ground sends its pulse back. Water mirrors it away and looks dark; rough ground, crowns of trees and broken ice scatter it back and look bright.

A change in that echo is a change in roughness or moisture. Flooding, harvest or ice movement are interpretations of it, and we label them that way. Each comparison stays inside one NISAR collection and CRID, so what moves is the ground, not the processing.
